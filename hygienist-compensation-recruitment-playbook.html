The problem

Most hygienist job ads fall into one of two traps

The flat-rate trap

A fixed hourly rate caps what a strong clinician can earn. Great work and minimal effort pay the same, so your best hire leaves for the next higher flat offer.

The commission trap

“Commission,” “quotas,” and “sales targets” read as retail pressure to a licensed professional. The candidates you want stop reading.

The fix

Floor + Accelerator

The Floor is a guaranteed W-2 hourly base. It never drops for cancellations, no-shows, or bad weather. The Accelerator pays the hygienist 30% of production above three times base pay. Thorough clinical care earns more. A slow day costs the hygienist nothing.

Every ad, script, and answer in the playbook explains that model in clinical language, not sales language.
