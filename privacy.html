Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This site collects as little information as it needs to sell products, book consultations, and answer questions. We do not sell your information.

What we collect

  • Purchases. When you buy from this site, Stripe and Link process your payment. We receive your name, email address, the billing details Stripe shares with sellers, and what you bought. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Consultations. When you book a call, Calendly collects your name, email, and any answers you give, and shares them with us.
  • Email. When you email us, we keep the correspondence to answer you and support your purchase.

What we do not collect

This site runs no analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own.

Third-party services

Pages on this site load content from services that may log your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Calendly runs the booking widget.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) plays the calculator walkthrough video.
  • Stripe and Link run checkout.

How we use information

To deliver your purchase, send receipts and download links, process refunds, schedule and hold consultations, and reply to you. We email you only about your purchase or booking unless you ask for more.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep purchase records as long as tax and accounting rules require. To see or delete the information we hold about you, email rk@brightlineops.llc.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
