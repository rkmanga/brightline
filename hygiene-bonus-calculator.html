Template · For dental practices

Model hygienist pay before you offer it.

The Dental Hygiene Production & Bonus Calculator is a Google Sheets template for one hygiene chair. Enter a base wage and collections. It returns the threshold, bonus, effective hourly rate, and labor cost as a percent of collections.

What it answers

Can you afford the offer, and is it competitive?

A flat hourly rate costs the same in a slow pay period as in a strong one. The calculator shows how a guaranteed base plus a 30% share of production above three times base keeps labor cost in line with revenue, and what the hygienist actually earns.

What’s inside

Four tabs, from offer to payroll

  • Tab 1

    Calculator

    Enter the hygienist’s base wage, hours, pay-period days, threshold multiplier, accelerator rate, labor target, and collections. Formulas return base pay, threshold, surplus, bonus, gross pay, effective hourly rate, and labor %.

  • Tab 1

    Model comparison

    Runs slow, typical, and strong production periods against a flat $65/hr offer, so you can see where flat pay overruns your margin.

  • Tab 2

    Pay Period Log

    Log each day’s hours and collections. Daily variance shows slow and strong days; the period summary reconciles the bonus for your payroll processor.

  • Tab 3

    Implementation checklist

    Four steps to roll the model out in your practice.

Edit only the input cells; calculated cells are locked to protect the formulas. Prefer Excel? Open your copy, then choose File → Download → Microsoft Excel.

FAQ

Questions

How do I get the template?

Buy it on Etsy or Notion Marketplace. Your download includes a one-page guide with a link that copies the calculator into your own Google Drive.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes, to make your copy. A free account works. You can then download it as an Excel file.

Does it handle more than one hygienist?

Each copy models one hygiene chair. Make another copy for each additional hygienist.

Is this payroll or tax software?

No. It models compensation and reconciles the bonus for each pay period. Your payroll processor still runs payroll and withholding.
